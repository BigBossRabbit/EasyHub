What was breaking, and why

Every feed died the same death. The site pulled RSS/GraphQL through browser-side CORS proxies (allorigins, codetabs, corsproxy, rss2json). Those are all dead: 522s, 401s, silent hangs. That's why the Chronicle showed nothing, HRF jobs spun forever, and bounties never loaded.
The Chronicle was fake. The "Africa Bitcoin news" hook rendered fabricated placeholder content, not real news. Rather than fake it better, it's gone.

The fix: one same-origin data file

A deploy-time script (scripts/build_feeds.mjs) now fetches everything server-side — StackerNews jobs, HRF careers, bounty-labelled GitHub issues, StackerNews news — and bakes it into public/feeds.json. The browser reads one local file. No proxies, no CORS, no 522s, nothing to break at runtime. A GitHub Action re-syncs it every 6 hours and redeploys.
